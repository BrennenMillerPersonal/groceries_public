Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

WFD? (“the app”) is built to keep your data on your devices and in your own iCloud. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that tracks you.

What stays on your device or in your iCloud

Your recipes, photos, ratings, meal plans, staples, grocery lists, stores and restaurant guides are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which Apple stores for you and the developer cannot read.

Sharing a grocery list

When you share a grocery list, the people you invite can read that list and tick items off it through iCloud sharing. You choose who is invited and can stop sharing at any time, which removes their access.

Sending a recipe in Messages

Recipes you send travel inside your message to the people you send them to. If a recipe has a photo, the photo is uploaded to the app's public iCloud storage under a random name, so the recipient's app can download it. That record contains only the picture: no recipe, name, or anything that identifies you. It can only be found with the link in your message, and your device deletes it after 30 days, or straight away if you cancel the send.

Things that never leave your device

  • Camera and scanning. Recipe scans are read by text recognition on your iPhone and, where available, Apple Intelligence on your device.
  • Microphone and speech. Voice commands are recognized on your device. No audio or transcript is sent anywhere or saved.
  • Location. A restaurant guide may use your location once to show how far each place is. It is kept in memory only and is never stored or sent.
  • Photo library. When you pick a photo, iOS hands the app only that photo.

Data collected by the developer

The only data that reaches storage the developer can access is the recipe photo described above, which is not linked to you, is used only to deliver the recipe, and is deleted after 30 days. The developer does not sell, share or use any data for advertising or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recipe, list or guide removes it from your devices and iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device, and you can remove its iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: support@example.com.
