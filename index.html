WFD? Support

WFD? keeps your recipes, plans the week's dinners, builds your grocery list and reads the steps aloud while you cook.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: email wfd.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

My recipes or grocery list aren't syncing to my other devices.

WFD? syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is turned on for WFD? in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Without iCloud, everything still works but stays on that device.

How do I share my grocery list?

On the Grocery tab, tap Household (the two-people button) and share your list from there. The people you invite can see the list and tick items off as they shop. Only you can build or change it, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Voice commands don't hear me.

Allow WFD? to use the microphone and speech recognition in Settings → Privacy & Security. Voice Commands is off each time you start cooking. Turn it on from the menu on the cooking screen, then say "next", "go back" or "repeat".

A scanned recipe has mistakes in it.

Scanning is done on your iPhone and can misread fractions or small print. A scan always opens as an unsaved recipe so you can fix it before you save.

A recipe someone sent me arrived without its photo.

Photos come with recipes sent from inside the app with Send in Messages, and are kept for 30 days. Recipes sent from the Messages app drawer, or opened after 30 days, arrive without the photo.
